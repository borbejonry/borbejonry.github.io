Inside the storybook

Little stories. Big feelings.

Join SKYE and her smiling strawberry plush as they explore everyday emotions in a safe, encouraging way. Read together, talk about the pictures, and practice at your child's pace.

Name the feeling

Explore happiness, sadness, anger, and fear—and remind children that every feeling is okay.

Practice calming down

Try a simple flower-and-balloon breathing activity together when everyone feels ready.

Ask for help

Practice simple words children can use when they need comfort or support from a trusted adult.

Read together

A few minutes can open a conversation.

Keep it cozy, playful, and pressure-free. Your child can point, talk, make a face, or simply listen.

  • Read one page at a time.
  • Ask, “How do you think SKYE feels?”
  • Try the breathing activity together.
  • Celebrate effort, kindness, and asking for help.

For parents and teachers 🌷

This storybook is an educational conversation starter, not therapy or a diagnostic tool. Follow your child's pace, offer reassurance, and seek individualized professional support if you have concerns about your child's wellbeing.

Helpful details

Questions caregivers may ask

What age is this for?

It is designed for preschool children around ages 3–5, with an adult reading alongside them.

How should I use the story?

Keep reading sessions short. Pause to talk about a picture, name a feeling, or practice an activity if your child is interested.

Does this replace professional support?

No. It is a gentle educational resource and is not a substitute for individualized professional advice or therapy.
